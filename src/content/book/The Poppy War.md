title: The Poppy War
author: R.F. Kuang
pubDate: 2023/07/14
---
The most interesting part about this book is the obvious parallel that can be drawn from the conflict in this book and imperialism in our world. At first I found this interesting to read after a few hundred pages I simply wished I was reading an actual history of imperialism. That isn't to say that fantastical allegories have no place, simply that this one did not captivate me with its fantastical elements.<br/>Kuang's prose also fluctuates between great and something I would read at a high-school writing group. The writing style is often at odds with the themes of the book. It deals with horrific scenes and yet the writing feels geared towards YA. I love this concept in general and I think blending fantasy with some of the worst periods in our history can give a different lens to view tragedies, I simply found the execution lacking.
