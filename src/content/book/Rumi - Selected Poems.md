author: Jalal ad-Din Muhammad ar-Rumi

<i><br/>When it’s cold and raining,<br/>you are more beautiful.<br/><br/>And the snow brings me<br/>even closer to your lips.<br/><br/>The inner secret, that which was never born,<br/>you are that freshness, and I am with you now.<br/><br/>I can’t explain the goings,<br/>or the comings. You enter suddenly,<br/><br/>and I am nowhere again.<br/>Inside the majesty.<br/><br/>---<br/><br/>My worst habit is I get so tired of winter<br/>I become a torture to those I’m with.<br/><br/>If you’re not here, nothing grows.<br/>I lack clarity. My words<br/>tangle and knot up.<br/><br/>How to cure bad water? Send it back to the river.<br/>How to cure bad habits? Send me back to you.<br/><br/>When water gets caught in habitual whirlpools,<br/>dig a way out through the bottom<br/>to the ocean. There is a secret medicine<br/>given only to those who hurt so hard<br/>they can’t hope.<br/><br/>The hopers would feel slighted if they knew.<br/><br/>Look as long as you can at the friend you love,<br/>no matter whether that friend is moving away from you<br/>or coming back toward you.<br/></i>
