Homer's Iliad and Odyssey are ingrained in the sands of time and stand as some of the most prolific pieces of literature to ever exist. Retelling these stories in a unique and satisfying way is an incredibly difficult job and I'm glad to say Stephen Fry has pulled it off fantastically.<br/><br/>For fans of his work, Fry's classic humour, wittiness and lyricism are all on display here. His hand seamlessly glides you through the tale all too quickly for my liking. For those too afraid or daunted to try Homer's works, this is a great starting point, for no matter how many times you've heard the tale, it can always be enjoyed once more. For those who are against the manner in which this story is told, I simply say that you can still read the original story. A retelling does not take away from a piece of work, it simply adds to it, and this is a welcome addition indeed.
