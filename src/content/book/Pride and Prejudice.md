---
title: Pride and Prejudice
author: Jane Austen
pubDate: 2023/05/27
---
A charming book. Austen's musings on life and the peculiarity of the individuals in the book are always insightful and often amusing. The story itself is rather straightforward but the characters are fantastically realised and I can easily see how readers can fall in love with this book. For me the story lacked dynamism but I will definitely reread this book later in the future.
