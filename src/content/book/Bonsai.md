---
title: Bonsai
author: Alejandro Zambra
pubDate: 2025/01/12
---
It’s interesting to read a book that seems to focus more on the detachment and isolation that love can bring than the pleasure and intimacy. It focuses on love and its sombre ephemerality, how it can be fleeting but also stay with you for your whole life, how the people you love fundamentally shape you even after they’ve left, how you’ll care for your bonsai for life and always remember.
