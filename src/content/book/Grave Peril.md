And it improves again!<br/><br/>These books have been getting better and better as I get through the series. The mystery in this novel is compelling and outright scary at times. There's enough layers to keep you guessing and the stakes feel incredbily high. I was pulled through the first 100 pages without even knowing it. <br/><br/>Dresden himself has even more issues this time around, we're introduced to another layer of complexity and danger regarding his past and we're slowly learning more about his childhood. I truly enjoy how Butcher tackles worldbuilding in this series. Nothing feels heavy-handed and there isn't any boring exposition. Instead the lore and story of the world are showedcased as we move through the story and the books in the series.<br/><br/>I can't wait to see what the next book has to offer. The ending left a couple loose threads that I'm hoping are explored.
