author: Agatha Christie

A thrilling and haunting book. It moves from thriller to a psychological horror as the inevitability of the situation dawns on the characters. Perhaps the most incredible achievement is that this mystery, containing ten main characters, is executed incredibly in 264 pages. A stunning book and a must-read for anyone who has a passing interesting in thrillers or crime.
