4.5*<br/><br/>A beautiful poetry collection. Tagore has a wonderfully unique writing style.<br/><br/><i><br/>“I am only waiting for love to give myself up at last into his hands.<br/>That is why it is so late and why I have been guilty of such omissions.<br/>They come with their laws and their codes to bind me fast; but I<br/>evade them ever, for I am only waiting for love to give myself up at<br/>last into his hands.<br/>People blame me and call me heedless; I doubt not they are right<br/>in their blame.<br/>The market day is over and work is all done for the busy. Those<br/>who came to call me in vain have gone back in anger. I am only<br/>waiting for love to give myself up at last into his hands.”<br/></i>
