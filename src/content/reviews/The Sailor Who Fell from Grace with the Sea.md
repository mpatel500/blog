---
title: The Sailor Who Fell from Grace with the Sea
author: Yukio Mishima
pubDate: 2024/04/17
---
An incredible book with a very disorganised and chaotic introduction. I cannot blame anyone for not sticking with the book, it's disjointed and the Mishima's writing style is not easy on the eye.<br/><br/>If you stick with it you'll find yourself rewarded with a harrowing but meaningful tale that will leave you wondering how we got here to begin with. I'd recommend giving this a read but don't expect it to be particularly easy, despite it's relatively short length.
