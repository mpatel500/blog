title: The Dragon Republic
author: R.F. Kuang
pubDate: 2023/07/25
---
As with the first book, the story is interesting but lack of execution and a generally bland writing style made it difficult to get through this one. I felt constantly close to reading a great book without ever quite getting there. I'll perhaps read one of Kuang's more recent books after this but I won't be reading the third book immediately.
