This sequel delves even further into the world of Middle Earth in a captivating way. The history of the land and its people are shown in full majesty in this book as Tolkien captures our hearts and minds with his avid and thoughtful descriptions. Every inch of this book exudes this feelings, from the songs that are sung to the tales of old that are told, this is a perfect example of how to build your world in a way that captures the reader.
