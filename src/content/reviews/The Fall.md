This book is framed as a discussion with the reader themselves. The story is told by Jean-Baptiste Clamence as he reflects upon his life and expresses his thoughts on his life and the world as a whole. Each chapter is packed with philosophical musings and an honesty towards his own situation which is beautifully devasting and tackle all manner of topics.<br/><br/>Books of this nature often challenge me to read but they seldom challenge me to examine my own life, as well as my way of thinking, this achieves both beautifully. I rarely stumble upon books in which I can say this, but <i>this books does what it intends to do in the most perfect way<i/>.
