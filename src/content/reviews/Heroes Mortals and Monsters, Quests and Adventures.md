A jaunty, jovial and generously charmingly rendition of the heroic greek myths you know and love. Whether you're new to greek mythology or a first-timer there's plenty to love here. What makes this version so readable is Fry's intense passion for the works. He clearly holds greek mythogy and mythological studies so close to his heart and it shows. Another great read.
