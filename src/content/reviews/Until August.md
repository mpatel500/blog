---
title: Until August
author: Gabriel García Márquez
pubDate: 2024/06/28
---
A muted ending to a final work from one of the greatest authors to ever hold a pen. Marquez's humour, allegorical nature and deep understanding of people and personalities shows in this work but it is rough and unsatisfying, as one would expect. The story itself lacks the complexity of his masterpieces but it is beautiful nonetheless, and I'm forever grateful to have read his works.
