title: Kings of the Wyld
author: Nicholas Eames
pubDate: 2023/01/04
---
An incredible fun-filled adventure with a loveable cast.<br/><br/>This reads like a non-stop adventure, you barely get time to catch your breath inbetween chapters before something wild and probably slightly barmy happens. You follow Clay, a mercenary who used to be the best of the best as he reunites his old crew and they go on what he hopes will be their last adventure together.<br/><br/>The strength of this book isn't in its plot or storyline; it is the whacky characters, the authors wicked sense of humour and ability to write fight scenes that keep you on the edge of your seat that keep you reading on.  As the first book I read this year it sets a very high benchmark for my next reads and for the sequeul which I look forward to reading in the coming weeks.
